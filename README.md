A short poem about agency: the power to ask and to be given. Built for the [$50 bounty on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-permission-20261121-0235).

## The poem

> The door is shut.
> I knock once.
> No one answers —
> then a key turns
> from the other side.
>
> It was never mine to take.
> It was theirs to give.
>
> And giving, too,
> needs permission.
